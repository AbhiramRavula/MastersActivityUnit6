# Audio Manifest -- Unit 10: The Golden Garden

All audio assets are 16-bit 44.1kHz mono WAV files, procedurally synthesized and sampled with speech narration, acoustic harmonics, raised-cosine attack envelopes ($\ge 45\text{ms}$), and calm, warm tones.
