# Unit 8: Washroom Etiquette ("AFTER YOU") — Pedagogical Skills & Learning Outcomes

**Curriculum Reference:** Senior Etiquette (Pages 32–33)  
**Target Class:** Class 3 & Class 4 (Ages 8–9)  
**Core Motto:** *"Leave it as you would like to find it."*

---

## 1. Primary Learning Objectives

By completing Unit 8, students demonstrate understanding and behavioral mastery of:

1. **Privacy & Patience (The Door):**
   - Recognizing that a closed door indicates occupancy.
   - Practicing polite knocking and waiting rather than banging, pushing, or rattling locks.

2. **Personal Hygiene & The 20-Second Rule (Handwashing):**
   - Understanding that proper hand hygiene requires both soap and water.
   - Scrubbing hands for the full 20-second duration (WHO/CDC recommended standard).
   - Visualizing how soap lather lifts germs away from skin.

3. **Water & Resource Conservation:**
   - Turning off taps firmly after washing to prevent water wastage.
   - Using only the necessary amount of paper towels.

4. **Public Area Empathy ("After You"):**
   - Disposing of paper towels directly into bins rather than dropping them on floors.
   - Wiping splashes off washbasins so the counter remains dry and clean for the next person.
   - Understanding that wet floors create slip-and-fall hazards for classmates.

5. **Proactive Civic Action (Community Responsibility):**
   - Identifying when shared facilities are out of supplies (e.g. empty soap dispenser).
   - Choosing to inform an authority figure (teacher/staff) to resolve the issue for the entire school community rather than ignoring it.

---

## 2. Social & Emotional Learning (SEL) Framework Alignment

| SEL Competency | Activity Implementation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Social Awareness** | Taking the perspective of the next student entering the washroom (**Meera**). |
| **Empathy & Compassion** | Recognizing how one's own carelessness (e.g. wet floor) causes direct distress or harm (slipping) to a peer. |
| **Responsible Decision Making** | Evaluating choices in Part 1 (Knock vs Bang) and Part 4 (Tell Teacher vs Just Leave) based on consequences. |
| **Self-Management** | Practicing impulse control when waiting for doors or completing 20 seconds of hand scrubbing. |

---

## 3. Formative Assessment & Star Rewards

Students earn up to **3 Gold Stars** during the activity:

- ⭐ **Star 1:** Completed 20 seconds of hand scrubbing with soap and water.
- ⭐ **Star 2:** Left the washroom clean, dry, and welcoming for Meera with all 7 inside steps followed.
- ⭐ **Star 3:** Reported the empty soap dispenser to a teacher so all students can wash their hands cleanly.

---

## 4. Classroom Discussion Prompts

At the conclusion of the activity, teachers facilitate discussion using the held prompt:

> **"Would the next person be happy entering this washroom?"**

**Follow-Up Extension Questions:**
- How does the "After You" principle apply in our classroom? (e.g. cleaning desks, pushing in chairs).
- How does it apply in the school cafeteria or dining hall? (e.g. returning trays, wiping crumbs).
- Why is telling a teacher about an empty dispenser an act of kindness for other students?
